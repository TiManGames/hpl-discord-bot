==Getting started==

*Get the tools from the section below
*Set up a [[HPL2/Development Environment|development environment]]
*Read about mod types in [[HPL2/Glossary|the glossary]]
*Set up a [[HPL2/TDD/Creating a Custom Story|Custom Story]] or a [[HPL2/TDD/Full Conversion Mod|Full Conversion]]
*If you are making an FC, reading about the [[HPL2/TDD/Config Files|config files]] will give you an idea about what can be done with the engine.
*[[Setting up an Online Repository|Set up an Online Repository]]
*Read the rest of the docs. They will give you a good idea what can be done and what will require fighting against the engine.
*Check the advice section before starting your first serious project
