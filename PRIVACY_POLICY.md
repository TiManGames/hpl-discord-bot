# HPL Discord Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **[OPERATOR NAME]** ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the HPL Discord Bot (the "Bot").

Before publishing this policy, replace all bracketed placeholders with the Bot operator's information, confirm the hosting provider's log settings, and have the document reviewed for the laws that apply to the operator and users.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to information processed by the Bot. Discord and the artificial-intelligence service providers used by the Bot process information under their own privacy policies, including [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacypolicy) and [SAP's Privacy Statement](https://www.sap.com/about/legal/privacy.html). The configured model provider may have an additional applicable privacy notice. This policy does not control their independent practices.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on how you interact with the Bot, we may process:

- **Discord account and server information:** your Discord username, display name or tag, user ID when supplied by Discord, server information, channel ID, thread ID, and message ID.
- **Message content:** questions that mention the Bot and follow-up messages posted in Bot-created or Bot-tracked threads.
- **Conversation context:** earlier user messages and Bot replies from the active thread, so the Bot can answer follow-up questions.
- **Technical and usage information:** timestamps, selected HPL game/channel mapping, model token counts, tool activity, request status, and diagnostic or error information.
- **Information you choose to provide:** any other information included in a message sent to the Bot.

Please do not send passwords, access tokens, financial information, government identifiers, health information, private source code, or other sensitive or confidential information to the Bot.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Receive and respond to HPL modding questions.
- Create and continue Discord support threads.
- Send relevant conversation context to the configured AI service.
- Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the Bot.
- Detect abuse and enforce the Terms of Service and Discord server rules.
- Understand performance, reliability, and service usage.
- Comply with legal obligations and protect users, the operator, and third parties.

## 4. AI Processing and Other Disclosures

To generate an answer, the Bot sends your question, relevant conversation history, system instructions, and selected documentation context through **SAP AI Core** to the configured model provider. Those providers may process the submitted information to deliver and secure their services under their applicable agreements and policies.

Information is also processed through **Discord**, because messages, threads, reactions, and replies are hosted on Discord. Discord server administrators and people with access to the relevant channel or thread may be able to view that content.

We may also disclose information:

- To hosting, infrastructure, security, and support providers that help operate the Bot.
- To comply with law, legal process, or enforceable government requests.
- When reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or prevent abuse.
- In connection with a merger, transfer, reorganization, or other change in control of the Bot, subject to applicable law.
- With your direction or consent.

We do not sell personal information or use Bot messages for targeted advertising.

## 5. Storage and Retention

The Bot does not currently use a persistent database for conversation history. Active thread history is held in the Bot process's memory and is cleared when the Bot process restarts.

This does not remove messages or threads stored by Discord. Discord content remains subject to Discord's retention behavior and the controls available to users and server administrators.

Operational console logs may contain Discord tags, channel or thread identifiers, message text, errors, and usage metadata. Their retention depends on the Bot's hosting and logging configuration. Before deployment, the operator should document and configure a specific retention period here: **[LOG RETENTION PERIOD, FOR EXAMPLE 30 DAYS]**. We may retain limited information longer when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies and the operator's service configuration.

## 6. Legal Bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the Bot at your request, for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Bot, with consent where requested, and to comply with legal obligations. The available legal bases and rights vary by location.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You can avoid further Bot processing by not mentioning the Bot and not posting in a Bot-tracked thread. You may be able to edit or delete your Discord messages using Discord's controls, subject to server permissions and Discord's practices.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, or an appeal. To make a request, contact us using the details below and identify the relevant Discord server, channel or thread, and message where possible. We may need to verify your identity. Some requests may be limited by applicable law or by information controlled independently by Discord or another provider.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 8. International Processing

Discord, SAP AI Core, the configured model provider, and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, the operator and relevant providers are responsible for using legally recognized safeguards for international transfers.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No system or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe information has been exposed through the Bot, contact us promptly.

If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to applicable requirements, contact us so we can review the request.
